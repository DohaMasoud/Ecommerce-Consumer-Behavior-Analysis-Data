# 🛍️ Ecommerce Consumer Behavior Analysis Report
## 📝 Project Overview
This project analyzes ecommerce customer behavior to reveal insights into purchasing patterns, 
customer demographics, loyalty levels, and decision-making behavior.
The analysis aims to drive business decisions that increase customer engagement, optimize sales strategies, and improve long-term customer value.

## 📈 Key Insights
6,945 total orders with an average value of $273 indicate strong demand.

Customers purchase about 7 times annually on average.

86% purchase accuracy highlights solid operational reliability.

Discounts increase purchase amounts (by ~4%) but not purchase frequency.

Online channels drive the highest revenue (34%), closely followed by mixed and in-store channels.

Customers who want or need a product spend more, while impulsive or planned buyers purchase more frequently.

PayPal users spend the most; Credit Card users buy more frequently.

High social media influence leads to higher purchase amounts; low influence customers are more loyal.

Spending peaks in Q3 (July–September) and spring months; dips in Q4 and early-year months.

Bachelor's degree holders and high-income customers are the top spenders.

Loyalty (3.03/5) and satisfaction (5.4/10) are moderate, showing room for improvement.

Customers spend little time researching, but take longer to decide, with a high CLV ($1,890).

## ✅ Recommendations
Promote premium products and recommend items based on past interest.

Increase order accuracy to 95% through better feedback loops and tracking systems.

Personalize offers and build communities to boost engagement and loyalty.

Use influencer campaigns to target high social media influence users.

Target discount-sensitive customers strategically rather than offering blanket discounts.

Optimize stock during peak months and boost sales in low months through promotions.

Improve product/service quality and gather customer feedback to enhance satisfaction.

Use email nudges and limited-time offers to shorten the decision-making process.
